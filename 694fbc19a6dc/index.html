Assumptions & notes
  • Planned-statement engine: menu sales = Σ(attach × menu price) × customers × open days × (1 + price increase); add-ons and catering use the same price uplift; ingredient costs are not inflated (3% waste allowance); payroll follows the staffing rule; card fees apply to all revenue; café sublease, café utilities/insurance (+3%/yr), café office (flat) and POS (+3%/yr); 21% federal + 8.25% Maryland tax with charitable deduction (ceiling − floor) and 80% NOL limit.
  • Shared inputs (menu prices/costs, open days, card %, staffing, tax, sublease, other income, master lease, church office / upkeep / marketing lines, Year 0 funding, equipment, POS) come from the input panels. The base comparison uses the same engine with the base menu mix (no add-ons, catering or price increase).
  • Structure assumed: a privately owned café C-corp pays Life Space a sublease (2,800 sf × $30.36 ≈ $85,000/yr) and carries its own utilities, insurance, café office and POS; Life Space keeps the master lease, church office and other building/ministry costs. The café is assumed to donate 100% of after-tax profit.
  • Tax assumptions: 21% federal + 8.25% Maryland; charitable gifts deductible above a 1% floor and up to 10% of taxable income; losses produce $0 tax and $0 donation, with an NOL carryforward (80% limit).
  • Rent from a non-controlled tenant is assumed excluded from Life Space unrelated business taxable income (IRC §512(b)(3)), assuming Life Space-owned equipment stays ≤10% of rent and Life Space provides no café-operating services. The sublease is assumed to be at documented fair-market rent (written sublease, usage schedule, non-conflicted board approval).
  • “Oct 7 comparison” rows in the tax panel use an earlier square-foot at-cost allocation method.
  • Café menu (menu v2, 2026-10-27): 64 menu items are grouped into the app categories. By default (Boyan, 2026-10-27) each category uses its planned sale price and unit cost (coffee $6.50 / $0.78, smoothies $9.75 / $1.90, pastries $3.40 / $0.95, sandwiches $8.20 / $2.30, quiche $7.60 / $2.30, soup $5.00 / $0.99, cake $8.00 / $1.80). The “Category prices from menu items” switch on the Café Menu tab replaces them with the mix-weighted averages of the items (edit item price, projected cost or mix %); with it off, each category shows what the items would give for reference. Costs marked EST are estimates without a quoted price. Quiche is a menu item at the app’s $7.60 / $2.30 (attach 0.08 in Year 1). All pastries are $4.50. The Turkey pesto panini replaces curry bread as the 4th sandwich (confirmed by Boyan). Soup ($5) and cake ($8) are categories sold at 10/day each (attach 0.0625 at 160 customers/day, held at 10/day in later years). With the switch on, the coffee category averages about $5.66 / $0.79 instead of the planned $6.50 / $0.78.
  • Break-even figures use the menu prices/costs with current attach rates; scaling customers scales all units proportionally. Required ticket = blended ticket needed at that volume.
  • The landlord tenant-improvement (TI) allowance is assumed to be reimbursed after spending, so there is timing risk. The base remodel ($70k) and the alternate $300k remodel path are separate alternatives and are not additive. The earlier $90k pre-opening figure overlaps the equipment lines.
  • All figures are planning estimates subject to CPA / exempt-organization attorney review.
